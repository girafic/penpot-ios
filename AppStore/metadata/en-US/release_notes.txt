Inspect your designs, see changes as they happen, browse a file's library, and switch between accounts.

• Inspect, as in Penpot's view mode: tap any layer to see its size, position and distances, its fills, strokes, shadows, blur and typography, and its CSS. Every value copies with a tap.
• Live: while a file is open, changes others make in Penpot appear within seconds, and you see who else has the file open. Comments refresh on their own.
• Assets: browse a file's components, graphics, colors and typographies, and its design tokens by set, resolved for the active themes. Open a component like a board, or copy a color, a typography's CSS or a token's value.
• Several accounts: add accounts on design.penpot.app or your own server, and switch between them without signing in again.
• Present on a big screen: connect a display or use AirPlay screen mirroring, and the prototype plays there full size while you tap through it on your iPhone.
• On wide screens, such as an iPhone in landscape, the board view lists the page's boards beside the board and shows Inspect next to it.
